Item 1

Sidebar: Artists, Playlists and Groups fold like nav items

  • Artists, Playlists and Groups look like the other nav items: a 22 px icon and the label. A chevron at the end opens or closes their list underneath.
  • Clicking the label opens the page: Artists → the Artists page; Playlists and Groups → the Library filtered to that kind.
  • Settings moves to the bottom, above your account. The web has no Settings page, so there only the account is at the bottom.
  • Search sits at the top (item 2).
  • Open and closed states are remembered on desktop, as today. The open lists scroll inside the middle of the sidebar; the top and the bottom stay put.

Item 2 · decisions C and D

Search anywhere

  • Opens from anywhere: the Search field at the top of the sidebar, Ctrl K, Ctrl F or /. It floats over the current page; Esc or a click outside closes it.
  • One list of matches, best first. ↑ ↓ move through it; the list scrolls to keep the selected row in view.
  • Tabs: All, Songs, Artists, Playlists, Groups, each with its count. ← → switch tabs, like the Claude CLI; clicking a tab works too. The selection goes back to the first row of the new tab, and a tab with no matches is dimmed and skipped.
  • In All, each row carries a small kind label (Song, Artist, Playlist, Group). The other tabs show only that kind, with every match, not just the top few.
  • Smart matching: case and accents don't matter. Whole matches come first, then words that start with what you typed, then matches anywhere, then close spellings ("tavrn" finds "Tavern Tracks"). Songs also match on their artist's name. The matched letters are highlighted.
  • Enter or a click opens the result's page. A song opens Songs, scrolled to it and highlighted for a moment; its ▶ plays it straight away (decision D). The search fields on Library and Songs go away (decision C).
  • Editing the text: since ← → switch tabs, the caret stays at the end; Backspace and typing work as usual, and Ctrl+A selects all.

Item 4

The artist field keeps its height when it opens

Today the note under the artist ("New artist · created when you save") disappears when the field opens for typing, so the row loses a line and the whole dialog jumps. The field gets a fixed one-line slot underneath that's always there: it shows the note, the "Use 'X'" hint, or "Did you mean…?", and stays empty (but keeps its height) otherwise. The orange line marks where the row ends in both states.
