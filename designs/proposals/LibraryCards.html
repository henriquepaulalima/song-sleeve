Library cards

The problem is the Library grid: songs, playlists and groups all use the same square card with a thin line icon, so you can't tell them apart at a glance. In this proposal each kind gets its own shape, not just a different icon. A song stays one sleeve, a playlist becomes a 2×2 mosaic of sleeves, and a group becomes a folder. The sidebar icons follow the same idea.

Details

  • Playlist mosaic: one tile per song, up to four. All four tiles share the same colour. A playlist with fewer songs shows plain tiles without the note ("Boss Fights"), and an empty playlist shows four plain tiles. When songs get cover art later, the tiles become the first four covers, the usual pattern in music apps.
  • Group folder: a plain folder whose front shows the playlist count.
  • Small kind icon before the SONG / PLAYLIST / GROUP label, so the kind is clear even in text.
  • The same art is used on Home, in the Playlist and Group page headers, and in the pickers.

Icons

The 2×2 grid moves from Library to Playlist, so Library gets "books on a shelf". The faded tiles are the current icons being replaced.
